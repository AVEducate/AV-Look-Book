03/First launchOne time only
Step 1

Download

Pick your machine above. The links always point at the newest version, and updates arrive inside the app when you're online.

Step 2

Open it once

The app isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so the very first open asks once.

On a Mac

Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway.

On Windows

SmartScreen says the publisher is unknown: click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS says the app is damaged? Paste this in Terminal and open it again: xattr -cr "/Applications/AV Look Book.app"

Step 3

Build your show

Quick Show Setup opens: name the show, add your destinations and presets. Every show saves itself to Documents › AV Look Book, one .avlb file per show.
