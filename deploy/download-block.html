First launch on a Mac

AV Look Book isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS stops the very first open. One time only: click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway.

If it says the app is damaged, paste this in Terminal and open it again: xattr -cr "/Applications/AV Look Book.app"

First launch on Windows

SmartScreen will say the publisher is unknown. Click More info, then Run anyway. One time only.

Your shows live in Documents › AV Look Book, one .avlb file per show, saved automatically. Questions: Info@aveducate.com
